*How far will one man go for his dog?*      

I went to Las Vegas back in March and while there we went to an area called Area 15 which has a [John Wick Experience](https://johnwickexperience.com/). Unfortunately we did not get a chance to do the John Wick Experience itself, but we were able to go in and have some drinks at The Continental bar and check out the gift shop. They have John Wick's classic car parked out front as well as the disposal van. Every employee was in character, and it literally felt like you had stepped into the John Wick universe. Suffice to say, I decided the following month to watch the films especially after seeing that all of them had been added to HBO Max. So I did like any sane person would, and I watched one John Wick film every night for five nights in a row. I had only ever seen the first film along with snippets of the others, and boy was I missing out.      

Today I want to talk about the films, and why I think the franchise is worth the watch. I will note that I watched in film chronological order and not on release date. This means that Ballerina is to be watched before the fourth film. I have not had the opportunity to watch The Continental show that is on Peacock, but I do believe that I have heard good things about it.    


So without further ado let's discuss the John Wick films.

# 🎥 John Wick: Chapter 3 - Parabellum 🎥     
**🗓️ Release Year:** 2019        
**🍿 MPA Rating:** R     
**⌚ Duration:** 130 Minutes    
                   
**📝 IMDB Description:** John Wick is on the run after killing a member of the international assassins' guild, and with a $14 million price tag on his head, he is the target of hit men and women everywhere. [See Full IMDB Page](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt6146586/?ref_=fn_t_4)  
                       
**🎬 Letterboxd Review:** Good action, continued learning more about the John Wick Universe     

John Wick kills a guy with a book 🤯       

**📼 Letterboxd Rating:** 3.7/5              
**🥤 IMDB Rating:** 7.4/10      
**✨ My Rating:** 4.5 ⭐      
                
**💭 Additional Thoughts:** There were some brutal death scenes in this film. We see the Casablanca Continental Hotel. Here we see how deep Wick's network runs. Technically because he was ex-communicado no one is allowed to help him; however, Wick is still able to leverage some connections and favors that people owe him. We also see the ballerinas for the first time when Wick visits the Ruska Roma theater academy. This also gives some more insight into Wick's past life. The defending of the Continental Hotel was phenomenal. We also see John Wick cut off his ring finger to prove his loyalty to the High Table.

## Future of the Franchise & Final Thoughts          
There is potentially a John Wick: Chapter 5 in the works. In my opinion, I don't think there should be another film specifically for John Wick. I think the franchise ends on a good note with the ambiguity of Wick's death. If Wick has to come back, I think it could be in another ballerina as a minor character or something like that. If they truly want to do another film, I think a pre-cursor film would be good. I do genuinely want to learn more about Wick's training, background, previous kills, etc.     

Overall I do think this franchise is well-done, and it's most certainly worth the watch and re-watches. I look forward to eventually trying the John Wick Experience.
