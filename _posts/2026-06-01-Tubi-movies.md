*There is a reason Tubi is free...*

If you aren't familiar with Tubi, it is another streaming platform that has the caveat of being free. Tubi has movies that aren't considered mainstream although every month it may have a few. It mainly has a lot of TV movies and straight to DVD movies. Similar to other streaming platforms, Tubi has Tubi Originals. Unlike some of the other streaming platforms, Tubi has the ability for someone to upload their own self-made movie on the platform.     
            
Here are some questions to ask yourself if you are looking for your big movie break:   
1. Do you have the desire to be a movie star? 🤩
2. Do you have friends with a camera? 📹
3. Do you have a computer and access to some free editing software? 💻
4. Do you have some microphones? 🎙️
5. Can you write a script? 📝
6. Can you read a script? 📖 

If you answered yes to these questions, then you can most certainly make a movie and put it on Tubi! No acting skills required.      

These are my opinions on some Tubi movies uploaded by aspiring directors. These are *not* Tubi Originals. This is not a dig at anyone who wishes to make a movie or anyone has produced a movie on Tubi. I am providing my thoughts and opinions on specific Tubi movies and not on the people in the movies.      

Today we will be talking about a few *gems* on Tubi that I think need to stay hidden.

## 🎥 4's A Crowd     
**📼 Genre:** Drama    
**🗓️ Release Year:** 2026        
**🍿 MPA Rating:** TV-MA          
**⌚ Duration:** 72 Minutes     
**📝 Tubi Description:** Being the third wife seemed like a dream, until a deadly house reveals loyalty has a price and only one woman will leave alive in the end. [View the Movie on Tubi](https://tubitv.com/movies/100052813/4-s-a-crowd)     
**🥤 IMDB Rating:** 5.8/10 -- 16 people rated it      
**📦 Letterboxd Rating:** NA    
**✨ My Rating:** 0.5 ⭐

**🎬 Letterboxd Review:** Where do I begin? This is close to a lifetime movie plot. The plot itself wasn’t too bad, but it definitely could use some improvement.      

For starters idk if it’s my TV or what but the picture is bigger than the screen which means the whole movie feels super zoomed in, and I missed half the texts sent.     

The audio mixing was mid. There were scenes of dead silence. Then sometimes the music was super loud while other times way too quiet. Towards the end, static can be heard as the mic cuts in and out.     

When they had their first meal together one of the wives, Netta, was talking, but the camera never once panned to her.     

Why did the living room not get painted to cover the patches of other color or at least a picture over them??     

The cutscenes are so frequent, and some of the shots are not Memphis. If you’re going to set the movie in a place and presumably film there, why use stock videos of other cities???      

*In regards to the plot (written as watched film):*    
Why did they not talk to the other wives about bringing a new woman in? What was the timeline of this relationship? We see them meet, have coffee, and then she’s already meeting them. She goes on a few more dates, and then all the sudden she’s engaged. What?? This was all within the first 19 minutes of the movie.     

Why you just got 13k in a Manila envelope lying around in your house? Are the people dumb? It’s so obvious that Amy doesn’t like Summer. It’s clear she wants her out of the relationship. Why are the other people so oblivious? Wait never mind Netta notices it, and they talk about it. While they’re talking there is a random sex scene in between their thoughts.      

At least tell Amy how to hold a camera 😭 also I don’t get why they don’t just kick her out of the house. It’s clear she is causing issues. Also what does Mook have that makes all these women want him? We barely see him. He also is a horrible mediator between the women.    

Spoke too soon he does have somewhat of a backbone when Amy tries to talk to him. Now they see how jealous Amy is.      

Unknown how many months later because the picture is too big for the screen.     

Neeta’s acting while sick wasn’t half bad. A little extra, but that’s alright. Why did she say slow down when she was already talking slow? Why didn’t they call an ambulance? Why did the driveway seem so long?      

How did Netta not hear Amy putting the big pills in her drink considering they made so much noise in the pill bottle. The blue tint over the interrogations was great. Why didn’t Mook or Summer say that Amy was jealous and doing all this stuff to her??? It’s obvious she has been poisoning Netta and blaming Summer.      

Is Mook stupid? Obviously he can see Amy’s jealousy and how spiteful she is. Yet he lets her manipulate him to think that Summer poisoned Netta.     

Oh yeah let’s kidnap her to get answers. What device did Amy use at Summer’s house(?)?? Also they said Netta was dead, but then we saw her in the hospital. The noise did not match her just having a breathing tube.     

Yeah let’s leave the psycho girl with the tied up woman. Mook are you stupid??? The stabbing scene was so goofy. Why is Amy still carrying around the medicine in her bag?      

Damn they didn’t even take Summer in. They listened to her statement standing outside the house. 💀    

Why was Maurice gripping Amy’s boobs like that? Was he in on the plan? What was the point of the flashback? Who’s this new chick at the end?      

So did Netta die?? We heard machines on at the hospital, but then they said she was dead. What was the ending?? It just ends and still feels unresolved.       

*Redeeming factor:*   
The movie is set in Memphis.
